About this site

A free, non-commercial fan project: Phish song statistics for people who want to know, in the middle of a show, how long it has been since a song was last played and what that means. Built and run by one fan for the community. It is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Phish, Phish Inc., their management, Phish.net, The Mockingbird Foundation, phish.in, LivePhish, or nugs.net.

What this site is, and is not

  • Free to use. Nothing is sold, and there are no accounts or subscriptions.
  • No advertising, no sponsored or compensated links, and no sale or sharing of traffic data.
  • No Phish music, audio, video, lyrics, or photographs. The site shows song titles, dates, venues, running times, and statistics computed from them.
  • Nothing is copied from phish.com. The live setlist panel uses only the song names and posting times from the band's public Bluesky posts; the text of those posts is not reproduced.
  • The setlist database is not offered for download. Every page links back to the source it draws from.

Data sources and attribution

Setlists, shows, songs, gaps
Courtesy of Phish.net, a project of The Mockingbird Foundation, via the Phish.net API v5 under its terms of use, which license non-commercial, attributed use. Phish.net's own show pages are linked from every setlist and performance shown here.
Recorded song lengths
phish.in, a community-run archive of audience recordings, via its public API. Shown with the tag "PI".
Official song lengths
The running times printed on LivePhish release pages, read once per show from the release page the band links to, and always linked back to that release so you can buy the show. Shown with the tag "LP".
Live setlists during a show
Song names and posting times from the public Bluesky posts of @phish.com, read through Bluesky's public API. Shown with the tag "BS" for post-to-post timing. Post text is not reproduced.

Privacy

Short version: this site sets no cookies, has no accounts, runs no analytics or advertising, and does not try to identify you. Here is everything it does touch, and why.

Cookies
None. Nothing is set by the site or by anything it loads, which is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
Browser storage
On a show day the landing page shows the weather at the venue. Your browser keeps the venue's map coordinates in its own local storage so they are not looked up twice. That is a city name and a latitude and longitude, nothing about you, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.
Server logs
Like every web server, ours records each request: your network address, the time, the page or data asked for, the browser you used, and the page that linked you here. Those lines live in the servers' system journal for 14 days, then age out. They are used only to keep the site working and to block abuse; they are never combined with other data, shared, or sold. The servers are in Oracle Cloud's San Jose, California region.
Requests your browser makes to others
  • Open-Meteo answers the show-day weather. The request names the venue's city, not your location (Open-Meteo's terms).
  • Bluesky supplies the live setlist during a show: your browser reads the band's public posts from Bluesky's public API (Bluesky's privacy policy).
Those two are the only third parties. The typefaces (Fraunces, Work Sans, and IBM Plex Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License) are served from this site, not from Google. Links to Phish.net, phish.in, LivePhish, and other sites are ordinary links; nothing is sent to them until you click.
Email
If you write to the address below, the message is kept as long as it takes to deal with it and is not used for anything else.
Your rights
There is no account to delete and no profile to export. If you believe the site holds something about you, or you want a log line removed inside the 14 days, write to the address below.

The site is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
